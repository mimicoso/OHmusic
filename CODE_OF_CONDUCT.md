# Code of conduct

OHMusic contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, respectful
and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the
people involved.
